# Agent Story Video

These are general bilingual captions for the infographics, not Facebook or LinkedIn post copy.

## English

Your AI agent tells the behind-the-scenes story of how it built your project, as a narrated video: real screenshots with private details blurred, a voice you pick from measured samples, word-timed captions, a cold open and music on the beat, in 16:9 and 9:16. Install the skill, give your agent the story, answer a few questions, and get your MP4s.

Get the skill and step-by-step installation guide:
https://github.com/eyoussef-builds/agent-story-video
